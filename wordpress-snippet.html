Good to know

Questions before you start.

Do I need to be fit before I start?+

No. Your coach starts from your current level and builds the plan gradually around what you can do now.

Can I request a female personal trainer?+

Yes. Share your preference and ETAD will recommend the most suitable available coach.

Where can I train?+

Sessions can take place at home, in your residential gym, outdoors or at another agreed location in Abu Dhabi.

Can training be adapted around pain or a previous injury?+

Share any limitations before the first session so ETAD can recommend an appropriately experienced coach. Medical clearance may be requested when needed.

Will I receive a standard weight-loss programme?+

No. The programme is shaped around your goal, current level, schedule and preferred training environment.

Do I need to commit before meeting the coach?+

No. The free first session lets you experience the approach and discuss the most sensible next step.
